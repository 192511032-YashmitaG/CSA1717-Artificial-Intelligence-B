TASK 7 – SOLVE THE RECURRENCE RELATION

Given T(0)=1 and T(l)=1+bT(l-1), expand by substitution:
T(l)=1+b[1+bT(l-2)] = 1+b+b²T(l-2).
Continuing gives T(l)=1+b+b²+...+b^l.
For b != 1, this geometric sum is T(l)=(b^(l+1)-1)/(b-1), which is Θ(b^l). For b=1, T(l)=l+1.

The substitution method applies because the recurrence expresses the cost at one depth limit in terms of the same cost at a smaller limit. In IDDFS, the total work through goal depth d is sum(l=0..d)T(l). Rearranging gives sum(i=0..d)(d-i+1)b^i. For b>1, the deepest levels dominate, so the total remains Θ(b^d), with more node visits than a single depth-limited search. This is the cost of revisiting shallow nodes to obtain the low-memory benefit.
